# M3: Ratings, schemes, roles, and fit

## Plan
1. Scheme model: offense and defense tendency sliders with ranges, the 4 + 4 named schemes, blends, and a tendency distance: proven by unit tests (shares sum to 1, blends interpolate, distance 0 to 1).
2. Roles and recipes: depth chart slots, each scheme's role per slot, rating weights summing to 1 stretched onto the overall scale and centered on the position's typical player, and trait adjustments: proven by role rating and trait tests, including the spec 7.3 zone versus power back example.
3. Abilities: trigger situations, a starter catalog of about 20 abilities with tiers and effects, estimated situation profiles from tendencies, ability value, and abilities for generated players: proven by ability value tests.
4. Fit: role rating capped at the overall plus or minus the league's fit cap (8) and at 0 to 99, so fit is always role rating minus overall, with a breakdown by ratings, traits, and abilities: proven by cap and breakdown tests.
5. Cohesion, coach abilities, coordinator mismatch, and coach flexibility: team schemes chosen from the staff at league creation: proven by unit tests.
6. Player page with the fit breakdown and a roster list to reach it: proven by Playwright tests at three sizes.

## Report
- Built: offensive and defensive tendency sliders; 4 offenses and 4 defenses with a role per depth chart slot; blends and scheme distance; 65 role recipes with rating weights and trait adjustments; 24 player and 10 coach abilities with triggers and effects; situation profiles estimated from tendencies; ability value; role ratings and fit bound by the league's fit cap (default 8) with a breakdown; cohesion, coordinator mismatch, and head coach flexibility; snap-ordered lineups; head coaches' schemes and abilities in new leagues (save format 2); a roster list and the player page with the fit breakdown.
- Evidence:
  - `npm run check`: pass, 232 unit tests. `npm run layout`: 40 passed in Chromium (WebKit runs in CI).
  - Spec 7.3 example: an agile, high-vision back rates 79 as a zone runner and 66 as a power runner; a power back rates 78 and 64 (tested both ways).
  - A typical player rates his overall in every role of his position; fit equals role rating minus overall, within the cap, for 150 random players in every role.
  - League with seed 5: 161 of 2,508 players have abilities (109 one, 45 two, 7 three); starters' fit averages +1.1 (5th to 95th percentile -4 to +6); cohesion runs -0.5 to +2.4; 32 lineups take about 100 ms.
- Reviews: the ui-reviewer found 10 problems (return focus and scroll, the Back button, 200% text, page titles, plate labels, fit colors, empty and unknown states); all fixed. The spec-reviewer found both done-when items met and 7 gaps (coach boosts for flaws, headline roles, lineup order, named strengths, the cap on role ratings, coach points counted twice, a tunable outside tuning.ts); all fixed.
- Calibration: not applicable (no sim yet; profiles are estimates until M4, D-7).
- Decisions: D-4 to D-7, with the scheme, recipe, and ability catalogs generated into DECISIONS.md.
