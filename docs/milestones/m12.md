# M12: Free agency, negotiation, and personalities

## Plan
1. Personality, morale, and the locker room (10.9): morale moved by role, snaps, results, pay against peers,
   and how teammates are treated; leaders, disruptive players, mentors who speed young players, and
   chemistry from time together: unit tests, the locker room effect metric.
2. The player decision model (11.7): offers scored on money against market value, contention, role,
   location, loyalty, coaches, and fit, weighted by personality, with waiting and discounts: unit tests.
3. Free agency weeks and bidding (11.8): offers through weeks 1 to 4, choices or waiting at each week's end,
   then open signing; AI bids by need and value toward the cap and the floor (D-33), replacing D-27's
   stand-in; the UDFA scramble on the same model: unit tests, economy metrics.
4. Negotiation (11.6) on the Free agency and Contracts screens: every term, counters that say what
   matters, patience, take it or leave it, and auto negotiation: layout tests.
5. Re-signing by value (D-38), tags priced from five years of cap percentages (D-39), holdouts and trade
   demands resolved without trades: unit tests.
6. Compensatory picks (11.8) and off-field events and suspensions (10.9, a setting): unit tests, pick counts.
7. Close: season records on chained seasons 3 to 20 (D-40); a 10-season unattended run keeps every team
   legal and competitive; economy metrics in range; reviews; report; Checkpoint B summary.
8. The user's additions, before the close: hidden floors behind the agent's ask and the front office's range
   (narrower with a better GM); contract structure valued by the player (guarantees by age and injury
   risk, cash up front, length by age and situation, incentives at their likelihood); free agency as a
   market (competing offers, waiting for better ones, prices falling from the first days, who's weighing
   offers); one negotiation for re-signings and extensions: unit and layout tests, the market metrics.
