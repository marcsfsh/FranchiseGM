# M5: Stats storage and history v1

## Plan
1. Full stat categories (spec 9.2): the sim records every missing field (first downs by player, air yards, 20+ plays, pressures, throwaways, yards after contact, broken and missed tackles, sacks and pressures allowed, run block wins, pancakes, solo and assisted tackles, coverage allowed, field goals by band, kickoffs, blocked punts, special teams snaps, starts, penalties by type), and each field belongs to one category table: proven by consistency tests (coverage allowed matches the offense, bands add up to totals).
2. Columnar season tables in IndexedDB (spec 9.3): one record per season per category with typed-array columns, game records (team stats, scoring, drives, recap) kept forever, and a player index built on load: proven by round-trip storage tests and a measured size per season.
3. Recording and running aggregates: games recorded in batches update player season and career totals, team season and franchise totals, league leaders, and the records book v1 (single game, season, career, and team records); preseason lines are stored but left out of totals: proven by tests that aggregates equal sums of the stored logs.
4. History travels with the league: export and import carry it, and deleting a league deletes it: proven by save tests.
5. Screens: the player page gains career stats by season and a game-by-game log for any stored season; History shows the records book: proven by Playwright at three sizes with an imported multi-season fixture, and a game log that loads in under 300 ms.

## Report
- Built: every spec 9.2 stat recorded by the sim (first downs by player, air yards, pressures and hits, yards
  after contact, broken and missed tackles, run block wins, coverage allowed, solo and assisted tackles, special
  teams snaps, starts, penalties by type); columnar season tables in IndexedDB with game records, player
  histories, season summaries with leaders, and the records book (D-14); history in exports and deletes;
  career stats and game logs on the player page; History v1 with the records book.
- Evidence:
  - `npm run check`: pass, 298 unit tests. `npm run layout`: 47 passed in Chromium (WebKit runs in CI).
  - Game logs load in about 20 ms from a three-season fixture (limit 300 ms).
  - Storage per season: about 40,700 stat rows, 0.94 MB of columns with ID lists (0.88 MB of typed arrays),
    0.18 to 0.24 MB gzipped; twice the spec's 20,000 rows (participation is 42%), but under the 0.5 MB packed
    estimate. Game records 1.6 MB, player histories 0.36 MB, records 170 KB.
  - Aggregates equal sums of the stored logs; a retried batch changes nothing; preseason and playoff games stay
    out of regular totals; export and import round-trip history.
- Reviews: the ui-reviewer found 11 problems, all fixed. The spec-reviewer found duplicate recording on retry,
  duplicate season records after trades, careers under the first team, ties and "broken record" noise, and
  hard-coded playoff weeks; all fixed with tests (D-16). Franchise totals and records moved to M17 (D-15).
- Calibration: the harness is M6. Season leaders run hot (sacks, receptions); M6 tunes them.
- Decisions: D-14 to D-16. Known gaps: leaders screen (M7); news for broken records (M7, M18); export and
  clear history (M22).
