# M5: Stats storage and history v1

## Plan
1. Full stat categories (spec 9.2): the sim records every missing field (first downs by player, air yards, 20+ plays, pressures, throwaways, yards after contact, broken and missed tackles, sacks and pressures allowed, run block wins, pancakes, solo and assisted tackles, coverage allowed, field goals by band, kickoffs, blocked punts, special teams snaps, starts, penalties by type), and each field belongs to one category table: proven by consistency tests (coverage allowed matches the offense, bands add up to totals).
2. Columnar season tables in IndexedDB (spec 9.3): one record per season per category with typed-array columns, game records (team stats, scoring, drives, recap) kept forever, and a player index built on load: proven by round-trip storage tests and a measured size per season.
3. Recording and running aggregates: games recorded in batches update player season and career totals, team season and franchise totals, league leaders, and the records book v1 (single game, season, career, and team records); preseason lines are stored but left out of totals: proven by tests that aggregates equal sums of the stored logs.
4. History travels with the league: export and import carry it, and deleting a league deletes it: proven by save tests.
5. Screens: the player page gains career stats by season and a game-by-game log for any stored season; History shows the records book: proven by Playwright at three sizes with an imported multi-season fixture, and a game log that loads in under 300 ms.
