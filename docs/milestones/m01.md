# M1: Base data (no-CSV path)

## Plan
1. Raw data: `tools/fetch-data.mjs` writes the name, surname, hometown, climate, and 2026 schedule files in `data-raw/` (schedule from nflverse's copy of the NFL feed, cross-checked game by game with ESPN); colleges are hand-written. Proven by loader tests: 272 games, 17 per team, one bye each, 8 international.
2. Teams, stadiums, and international venues with climate normals in `src/data`: proven by data tests (32 teams, 8 divisions, every stadium field, 12 months of climate).
3. Rule set with 2026 defaults ($301.2M cap, roster and salary rules): proven by rule set tests; the cap figure is recorded as a decision.
4. Player model (ratings, traits, positions), hand-set overall formulas, and a least-squares fitter: proven by tests (0 to 99 range; the fitter recovers known weights from the fixture).
5. Madden CSV import (declarative mapping, validation, transforms, `docs/MAPPING.md`) against a synthetic fixture with provisional headers: proven by import validation tests.
6. Player generator (provisional `archetypes.json`, names, colleges, hometowns, traits, personality): proven by distribution and determinism tests.
7. Staff and owner generator and the fictional league (53 + 16 per team, about 300 free agents, contracts under the cap): proven by league tests.
8. `tools/build-db.ts` and the embedded base database, decoded at startup: proven by a Playwright test that it loads in under 1 second.

## Report
- Built: data fetch tool and data-raw files (SSA first names, Census surnames and places, NOAA climate normals, the 2026 schedule, hand-written colleges); 32 teams, 30 stadiums, 8 international venues; the rule set with 2026 defaults; the player model, hand-set overall formulas, and a least-squares fitter; the Madden import with a declarative mapping, validation, and MAPPING.md; the player, staff, and owner generators; the fictional league; tools/build-db.ts and the embedded base database.
- Evidence:
  - `npm run check`: pass, 167 unit tests. `npm run layout`: 28 passed in Chromium (WebKit in CI).
  - Base database: 477 KB of plain JSON in game.html, ready about 35 ms after navigation starts (test limit 1 s).
  - Schedule: 272 games match between nflverse and ESPN game by game; the only differences are ESPN's midnight placeholders for 24 flex-eligible late-season games, kept at the NFL's 1 p.m. default.
  - Import on the fixture: 211 rows, 208 imported, 3 rejected (missing name, unknown position, duplicate), 8 values fixed; roster-count checks flag the fixture's six-player teams.
  - Fit on the fixture, mean absolute error: QB 0.29, WR 0.28, LT 0.38, LE 0.23, CB 0.72 (97 to 100% within 2 points).
  - Fictional league: 53 + 16 per team, 300 free agents, every team at 90 to 98.5% of the $301.2M cap, no repeated names, veteran limits held across seeds; generated in about 250 ms.
- Calibration: not applicable (no simulation yet).
- Decisions: D-2 (2026 cap and pay minimums).
- Known gaps: the OVR-versus-Madden check and M11's archetype check wait for the CSV (docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md). Default staff and owners are embedded for real-data leagues; fictional leagues generate their own. NFL.com renders its schedule in the browser, so the schedule came from nflverse's copy of the NFL feed, cross-checked with ESPN.
