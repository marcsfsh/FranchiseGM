# M1: Base data (no-CSV path)

## Plan
1. Raw data: `tools/fetch-data.mjs` writes the name, surname, hometown, climate, and 2026 schedule files in `data-raw/` (schedule from nflverse's copy of the NFL feed, cross-checked game by game with ESPN); colleges are hand-written. Proven by loader tests: 272 games, 17 per team, one bye each, 8 international.
2. Teams, stadiums, and international venues with climate normals in `src/data`: proven by data tests (32 teams, 8 divisions, every stadium field, 12 months of climate).
3. Rule set with 2026 defaults ($301.2M cap, roster and salary rules): proven by rule set tests; the cap figure is recorded as a decision.
4. Player model (ratings, traits, positions), hand-set overall formulas, and a least-squares fitter: proven by tests (0 to 99 range; the fitter recovers known weights from the fixture).
5. Madden CSV import (declarative mapping, validation, transforms, `docs/MAPPING.md`) against a synthetic fixture with provisional headers: proven by import validation tests.
6. Player generator (provisional `archetypes.json`, names, colleges, hometowns, traits, personality): proven by distribution and determinism tests.
7. Staff and owner generator and the fictional league (53 + 16 per team, about 300 free agents, contracts under the cap): proven by league tests.
8. `tools/build-db.ts` and the embedded base database, decoded at startup: proven by a Playwright test that it loads in under 1 second.
