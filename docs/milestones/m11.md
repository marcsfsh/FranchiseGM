# M11: Player generation, scouting, and the draft

## Plan
1. Draft classes (10.3, 22.4): each class made as the season before its draft starts, about 450 prospects
   by a position mix, with class and position strength draws and bust and gem spreads (true against
   perceived value), from a generator that can make a prospect years before his class (post-M23 1.1):
   unit tests for size, mix, strength, spreads, and unique names (10.1).
2. Draft picks: every team's picks for the next three drafts as records M15 can trade, the order by
   finish, and compensatory picks: unit tests.
3. Scouting (10.4): grades as true value plus noise that shrinks with scouting points and the director's
   skill, scouts earning weekly points by region, spent by the user or on auto, trait and ability
   reveals; the combine and pro days; top-30 visits; media hype, a media big board, and weekly mock
   drafts from midseason: unit tests.
4. The Scouting screen (prospects, the big board, assignments, combine results): layout tests, ui-reviewer.
5. The draft: 7 rounds plus compensatory picks, the user picking or on auto, the AI's need-and-value
   model, rookie deals by pick, media grades; the Draft room screen: unit and layout tests, ui-reviewer.
6. The UDFA scramble: offers, and rookies choosing by money and roster opportunity until M12: unit tests.
7. Close: draft hit rates by round over a 20-season chained run against sourced targets; reviews; report.
