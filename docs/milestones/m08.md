# M8: Rosters, contracts, and cap v1

## Plan
1. Cap accounting (spec 6.5, 11.1 to 11.3): cap hits with per-game bonuses and incentives, dead money on
   release or trade, June 1 designations, restructures, void years, guarantees (full, injury only, vesting),
   options, and the team cap sheet with rollover and the offseason top 51: proven by golden tests.
2. Roster rules (spec 12.1): 53 and 90, practice squad size and veteran limit, elevations (2 a game, 3 a
   season each), injured reserve, PUP, NFI, and suspended lists, and waivers with claim priority: proven
   by unit tests that each illegal move is refused with a reason.
3. Transactions (spec 19.4) that validate first and preview their cap effect, in-season signings with a
   simple acceptance model, and AI moves through the same functions: proven by a full season in which every
   team stays legal and under the cap every week.
4. Screens: transactions and free agents with cap preview dialogs, the cap sheet, and the contract view
   (cap hit, cash, dead money before and after June 1, savings, restructure): layout tests at three sizes
   and a test that the UI can't reach an illegal state.
5. Close: a season with the user's roster moves, the full layout suite, and calibration if AI moves shift
   results.

## Report
- Built: cap accounting from contract structure (spec 11.2): base by weeks in force, bonuses when earned,
  prorated signing, option, and restructure bonuses with void-year acceleration, incentives settled on
  regular-season totals, and dead money from releases, trades, claims, and declined options, split from June 2
  (a June 1 designation holds the full charge until then); the cap sheet with the offseason rule of 51.
  Roster rules (spec 12.1): 53 and 90, the practice squad and its veteran limit, elevations, reserve lists,
  and waivers with claim priority. One checked transaction module with cap previews (spec 19.4) shared by
  the screens and the AI, and a simple free agent acceptance model. Screens: Free agency with offers and the
  waiver wire, the Salary cap sheet by year, the contract card, and roster moves; waiver inbox and news items.
- Evidence:
  - `npm run check`: pass, 433 tests. `npm run layout`: 71 passed in Chromium (WebKit runs in CI).
  - Golden tests for proration, dead money, June 1 cuts and the June 2 hold, void years, restructures,
    guarantees, options, incentives, claims, and cash; unit tests refuse each illegal move with a reason.
  - A full season keeps every team legal and under the cap every week, with elevations inside their limits;
    layout tests show the UI refusing an offer on a full roster and a third elevation.
  - Reviews: the ui-reviewer's 18 findings and the spec-reviewer's 12 are fixed.
- Calibration: replays make no roster moves; the seed 1 100-season run matches M7 metric for metric (C-18).
- Decisions: D-21, D-22.
- Known gaps: cap growth, rollover, and the salary floor wait for M10; extensions for M12; the international
  practice squad exemption isn't modeled (docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md).
