# M8: Rosters, contracts, and cap v1

## Plan
1. Cap accounting (spec 6.5, 11.1 to 11.3): cap hits with per-game bonuses and incentives, dead money on
   release or trade, June 1 designations, restructures, void years, guarantees (full, injury only, vesting),
   options, and the team cap sheet with rollover and the offseason top 51: proven by golden tests.
2. Roster rules (spec 12.1): 53 and 90, practice squad size and veteran limit, elevations (2 a game, 3 a
   season each), injured reserve, PUP, NFI, and suspended lists, and waivers with claim priority: proven
   by unit tests that each illegal move is refused with a reason.
3. Transactions (spec 19.4) that validate first and preview their cap effect, in-season signings with a
   simple acceptance model, and AI moves through the same functions: proven by a full season in which every
   team stays legal and under the cap every week.
4. Screens: transactions and free agents with cap preview dialogs, the cap sheet, and the contract view
   (cap hit, cash, dead money before and after June 1, savings, restructure): layout tests at three sizes
   and a test that the UI can't reach an illegal state.
5. Close: a season with the user's roster moves, the full layout suite, and calibration if AI moves shift
   results.
