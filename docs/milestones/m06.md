# M6: Calibration harness v1

## Plan
1. Replay core in `src/engine/calibration`: generated leagues replay the 2026 regular season with seeded
   streams and yield compact game, team, player, and injury facts, plus a fit experiment that gives each
   starter his best or worst scheme fit at random: proven by unit tests (fixed seeds replay exactly; facts
   match the box scores).
2. Metrics, targets, and reports: games and seasons (home field, favorites by rating gap, margins, win
   spread and extremes), league stats, leaders and shares, injuries, and effect sizes (fit, weather);
   `calibration/targets.json` with sourced pass and warn bands and wide CI bands; Markdown and JSON reports
   that mark each metric pass, warn, fail, or not measured yet: proven by unit tests on synthetic samples.
3. Runner: `npm run calibrate -- --seasons N` spreads replays over worker threads, writes the report to
   `calibration/reports/`, and prints the summary; CI runs 20 seasons with the CI bands: proven by a run.
4. Tuning: 100-replay runs with one-file changes to `src/engine/tuning.ts`, each logged in
   `docs/CALIBRATION.md`, until game and stat metrics pass: proven by the final report.
5. In-app runner (spec 23.1): a calibration job in the worker and a hidden dev menu in Settings (seven taps
   on the version, always on in the debug build) with a progress bar and the report: proven by a layout test.
