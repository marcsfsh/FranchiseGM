# M10: Offseason engine and schedules

## Plan
1. Sortable tables (post-M23 1.2): one shared component for every table, sorted by up to four columns
   from the headers or a Sort panel: unit tests for the order, layout tests for the headers, the
   announcement, focus, and the panel at 320 pixels with 200% text.
2. Changes with causes (post-M23 1.1, post-M42 1.1): every rating change goes through one function with
   its cause, and roster and depth chart changes are logged as events with reasons: unit tests.
3. Schedules for 2027 on (spec 5.2): rotations from the 2026 seed year and a week grid: unit tests over
   a 12-year window.
4. The offseason calendar (spec 4.1): every phase from staff through final cutdown, the new league year
   (rollover, cap growth, carryover), expirations, retirements (10.7), a stand-in rookie class until
   M11's draft, and the next season: unit tests per phase, a layout test through an offseason.
5. Progression and regression (10.5) with the curve tables and speed sliders, and training focus
   (weekly and offseason) with auto: unit tests, layout tests for Settings and training.
6. The re-sign window (11.4, 11.5): extensions, tags, RFA tenders, 5th-year options, for the user and
   the AI: unit tests for amounts, a layout test.
7. OTAs, training camp, preseason, and final cutdown: unit tests, a layout test.
8. Close: seasons chained through the weekly loop with aging metrics, a 10-season run, reviews, report.

## Report
- Built: sortable tables; rating changes with causes, roster and depth events with reasons; 2027+ schedules;
  the offseason a step at a time (the new league year's cap, pay scales, and salary floor; retirements;
  stand-ins for free agency and the draft); progression with its settings and the Training screen; the
  re-sign window on the Contracts screen and Settings > Automation; OTAs, camp battles on the depth chart,
  preseason games, the cutdown; accrued and credited seasons; contract records in player histories.
- Evidence:
  - `npm run check`: pass, 507 tests; schedule tests cover a 12-year window. `npm run layout`: 119 passed
    in Chromium (WebKit runs in CI).
  - Full calibration: seeds 1 and 2 each pass 76 of 76 targets, the aging ones over three 10-season
    chained leagues each.
  - Reviews: the ui-reviewer's findings (Training 14, Contracts 13) and the spec reviewer's 9 are fixed;
    the user's six notes on the decisions became D-35 to D-40.
- Sizes and times (`npm run measure`): game.html 1.61 MB; a new league 0.28 s; one game 34.6 ms; a regular
  season 17.6 s; its save 6.54 MB as JSON, 0.77 MB gzipped.
- Calibration: C-20 to C-23. Decisions: D-24 to D-40. Save format 16.
- Known gaps: stand-ins until M11 (the draft) and M12 (free agency, negotiation, re-signing by value,
  tag pricing: D-38, D-39), so teams pay about 75% of the cap in cash, short of the floor; season records
  move to chained seasons in M12 (D-40); no Pro Bowls for option tiers until M17; no PUP or NFI placement.
