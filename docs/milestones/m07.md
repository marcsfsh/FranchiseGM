# M7: Season loop

## Plan
1. Season state and standings: finished games, NFL division and wild card tiebreakers with a seeded coin
   toss, 7 seeds per conference: proven by tiebreaker and seeding unit tests on built seasons.
2. Weekly advance in the worker: sim the week, record stats, heal injuries and set weekly designations
   (out, doubtful, questionable, probable), lingering effects, re-injury risk, playing hurt, autosave:
   proven by unit tests (deterministic week, statuses, a hurt starter sits or plays hurt).
3. Playoffs: Wild Card through the Super Bowl with re-seeding, higher seed hosting, and a rotating neutral
   Super Bowl site: proven by a test that plays week 1 through the Super Bowl to one champion.
4. AI weekly management on the decision framework (spec 14.1): auto depth charts by coach personality
   (12.2) with packages and rotations (12.3), injury signings from free agents, opponent-tailored game
   plans v1 (8.7): proven by unit tests (legal rosters every week, plans that differ by opponent).
5. Depth chart and game plan screens with auto toggles and keyboard moves (style 7.4): layout tests.
6. Advance control, pause rules and inbox v1 (19.6), news feed v1, players of the week, standings card:
   layout tests and news unit tests.
7. A full season on auto and with manual changes: an integration test and a layout test.

## Report
- Built: the season from week 1 through the Super Bowl in the worker (standings, NFL tiebreakers with a
  seeded coin toss, 7 seeds, re-seeded playoffs, a rotating neutral Super Bowl site); injury updates between
  games with designations, lingering effects, and playing hurt; AI weekly management on the 14.1 framework
  (depth charts by coach profile, rotations and five situational subs, questionable calls, injured reserve
  and signings, opponent game plans from a scouting report, all logged); depth chart and game plan screens
  with auto toggles, keyboard moves, packages, and a rest control; the advance control with pause rules,
  inbox v1, news feed v1, players and rookie of the week, and standings and playoff picture cards.
- Evidence:
  - `npm run check`: pass, 385 unit tests. `npm run layout`: 68 passed in Chromium (WebKit runs in CI).
  - Unit tests play week 1 through the Super Bowl on auto and with the user managing the lineup and plan;
    11 tiebreaker and seeding tests pass.
  - Reviews: the ui-reviewer's 13 findings and the spec-reviewer's 9 (lost mid-sim edits, stale screens, no
    rest control, reserve counted in weeks, missing subs and rookie award, loose tunables, thin tiebreaker
    tests) are fixed.
- Calibration: 100 seasons on seed 1 and on seed 2: 61 pass, 1 warn, 0 fail (perfect or winless teams 6.0
  per 100 seasons on seed 1, receptions leader 139 on seed 2), after the replays dressed backups for hurt
  players and the passing game was retuned (C-16, C-17).
- Decisions: D-20.
- Known gaps: plans v1 have one run and pass balance, the user's team makes no roster moves until M8, and
  awards are weekly only (docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md).
