   the bracket): unit tests for the explanations, layout tests at three sizes.
2. Schedule and results by week and by team, and a game page (spec 8.8): box score with team totals and

   home, the inbox, and game logs: layout tests open a played game at three sizes.
3. League stats (spec 19.3): season and career leaderboards with category, position, and team filters, and
   team stats: unit tests for the leaderboards, a layout test.
4. News and inbox pages; home gets its quick actions (advance, set lineup, open offers), a cap summary, and
   a featured player (style guide 4.4, 7.6); team pages for other clubs (roster and results): layout tests.
5. Settings: the sim sliders (spec 22.3) with user and AI values and a reset: a unit test that a slider
   reaches the sim, a layout test.
6. Close: a Day and Night sweep of every screen at three sizes, a full season played on a phone-sized
   screen, the ui-reviewer on new screens, the spec review, then Checkpoint A.
