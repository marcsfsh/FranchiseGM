# How to play (Checkpoint A build)

Franchise GM is one file: open `dist/game.html` in a current browser on a computer, tablet, or phone. It
needs no network. Leagues save in the browser as you play; export them from Settings now and then, since
some browsers (Safari especially) clear site data.

## Start a league
Open Leagues, choose New league, name it, pick your team, and create it. The league is fictional: every
roster is generated, and the 2026 schedule is the real one.

## Play a season
- The team hub leads with your next game. Play week plays one week; Sim to the playoffs and Sim through
  the Super Bowl play many. A multi-week sim stops for the events you pick under Settings > Pause the sim for.
- Set lineup opens the depth chart. Leave it to your head coach, or take it over and move players up and
  down each slot with the Up and Down buttons.
- Game plan shows the scouting report on your opponent. Hand the plan to your coordinators, or set the run
  and pass balance (overall, by down and distance, in the red zone, and in the two-minute drill), the
  defensive calls, and a player to feature, shadow, double, or spy.
- Your inbox collects your results, injuries, awards, and waiver news; each result links to its box score.

## Manage the roster and the cap
- Roster lists your players; open one for ratings, fit, contract, and career stats, and use Roster moves to
  release, restructure, move to or from injured reserve, promote, or elevate.
- Free agency lists free agents with their asking price and the waiver wire. Every move previews its cap
  and roster effect first, and the game refuses moves the rules don't allow, with the reason.
- Finances opens the salary cap sheet by league year.

## Follow the league
League has the standings (with the tiebreakers explained), the playoff picture and bracket, the schedule
with box scores, drives, and recaps for every game, stat leaders for the season or careers, and the news.
Club names in the standings open each team's roster and schedule. History holds the records book.

## Settings
Day and Night, the layout (phone, tablet, desktop, or automatic), density, the pause rules, and the game sim
and stat sliders (100% is the calibrated league). Save, export, or switch leagues from here too.

## What this build doesn't do yet
The season ends after the Super Bowl: the offseason (draft, free agency, re-signing, a new season) arrives
with Phase 2. Staff, Scouting and draft, and Trades show placeholders. docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md lists the rest.
